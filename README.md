# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn & Business Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes telecommunications customer data to uncover key trends in **customer churn (attrition)**, **service adoption**, and **revenue performance**. By cleaning raw customer records and building an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard, this analysis identifies high-risk customer segments and provides actionable insights to improve customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
* **Understand Churn Drivers:** Identify why customers leave based on tenure, contract type, payment method, and monthly charges.
* **Track Core Telecom KPIs:** Monitor total subscribers, overall churn rate, active vs. churned users, and monthly revenue.
* **Segment High-Risk Customers:** Highlight which plans and demographic segments require targeted retention offers.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Microsoft Excel / Power Query:** Data cleaning, handling missing values, duplicate removal, and formatting.
* **Power BI (DAX & Data Modeling):** Interactive dashboard design, custom KPI cards, slicers, and visual storytelling.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings
* **Contract Type Impact:** Month-to-month subscribers show a significantly higher churn rate compared to customers on 1-year or 2-year contracts.
* **Early Tenure Risk:** Customers within their first 6–12 months of service represent the highest proportion of attrition.
* **Pricing & Usage:** Higher monthly charges without bundled value-added services correlate strongly with customer drop-offs.
